# Competitor design pattern and the NORA pattern interrupt

Source: 7 rackets in `competitors/` (Pallap Evoforce Pro, Xcalion Unum, Bullpadel Hack 05, NOX AT10 Genius Xtreme, Quad Shark,
Adidas Metalbone HRD+, Adidas Cross It Light).

## The pattern (what all seven do)
1. Giant centred logo on the hitting face, drilled through by the holes (7 of 7).
2. Front and back identical (as far as these product shots show; all are sold as symmetric designs).
3. Left-right mirror symmetry, except the logo itself (6 of 7; Bullpadel adds one thin diagonal line).
4. Base is black or white (6 of 7); only Quad uses a colour (olive) and only Adidas Cross It uses a pastel.
5. One accent colour, usually neon or gold (lime, cyan, red, gold, orange).
6. Model name in small caps in a band between the holes and the throat (6 of 7).
7. Tech-spec icon clutter near the throat (NOX, Adidas, Pallap, Bullpadel).
8. Hole grid is plain and uniform; graphics ignore it instead of using it.
9. The frame is passive: a pinstripe at most.
10. Finishes are used for decoration (a holo logo, a gold line), never to tell the player something.

## The interrupt: break the five strongest rules at once
| Their rule | NORA breaks it |
|---|---|
| Giant centred logo | No logo on the face. The hero is the two-face idea. NORA appears small on the frame edge and butt cap |
| Front = back | Front and back are opposites: one light, one dark; one matte, one mirror |
| Mirror symmetry | One off-centre element per face that continues over the edge onto the other face |
| Black or white + neon | A colour pair nobody has: bone/sand white with deep oxblood, foil instead of neon |
| Holes ignored | The drilling IS the graphic: hole rims ringed in foil on one face form the Gemini constellation |
| Spec clutter | Zero icons. One line of text only |
| Passive frame | The frame carries the brand and the wrap-around stripe |

## Concept: NORA Gemini "Eclipse"
* Front "Day": matte bone-white lacquer over the whole face (3D + Matte in the sweet spot), no logo. A single oxblood gloss band enters
  from the right edge at 58 deg and stops at the sweet spot. Hole rims plain.
* Back "Night": gloss 16K aluminised carbon, visible weave, near-black. The same band arrives from the opposite edge in gold hot-stamp
  foil, so the band is one ribbon wrapped round the racket. Eight holes are ringed in holographic laser foil and joined by hairlines:
  the Gemini constellation, drawn with the drilling.
* Frame: half bone, half black, split exactly at the edge centre line; "NORA" debossed once at 2 o'clock on the rim.
* Text: only "I" and "II" in the throat, 6 mm high.
