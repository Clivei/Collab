# NORA Gemini "Prism" - front and back

Two faces = the two sides of the racket. Front (Face I, attack) and back (Face II, control).
No face is split into halves: each face has ONE base surface over the whole face, and the other effects sit on top in fixed zones.
Both faces share the same zones and logo position, so they read as one racket.

## Shared zones (same on both sides)
| Zone | Effect |
|---|---|
| 13 mm border round the face | Shiny (mirror bezel) |
| Sweet spot | 3D Hexagon with Sand Grit |
| NORA wordmark, upper head | Laser (holographic) |
| One thin Gemini stripe at 58 deg | Hot stamping foil |
| Throat bridge | 3D + Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible |
| Frame tube | Matte black with a gloss pinstripe in the face colour |

## Front - Face I "Attack"
Base surface: 3D + Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible, crimson-tinted 3K twill. Gold foil stripe, warm holo NORA (gold > magenta > cyan),
silver "I / GEMINI". Crimson pinstripe on the frame.

## Back - Face II "Control"
Base surface: Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible, cobalt 16K aluminised, smooth (finer sand in the sweet spot). Silver foil stripe,
cool holo NORA (ice blue > violet > silver), gold "II / CONTROL". Cobalt pinstripe on the frame.
