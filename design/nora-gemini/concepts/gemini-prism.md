# NORA Gemini "Prism" - all nine factory effects on one racket

## Concept
One diagonal cut (58 deg, the Gemini line) splits each face into two finish zones. Every effect on the factory sheet appears once,
each in the place where it does a job:

| Zone | Effect | Why it sits there |
|---|---|---|
| Strike zone (sweet spot) | 3D Hexagon + Sand Grit | Grip and spin where the ball lands; no foil to wear off |
| Upper diagonal field | 3D + Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible | Shows the 3K weave, catches light on the swing |
| Lower diagonal field | 3D + Matte | Calm, dark counterweight to the gloss field |
| Face border (13 mm) | Shiny | Mirror frame around the textured centre |
| Cut line | Laser Gold Hot Stamping stripe | The hero line; follows the cut |
| NORA wordmark | Laser (holographic) | The one hero graphic per face |
| Small type, Roman numeral | Silver Hot Stamping | Secondary, quiet |
| Throat / bridge | Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible | Structure stays honest |
| Frame tube | Matte black with a gloss red pinstripe | Contrast of finish, not colour |

Palette 60/30/10: carbon black / crimson red #D11F3F / foil (gold + holo).

# Face II "Prism - Control" (the other face)

## Concept
Same cut, same border, same logo position, mirrored so the two faces read as one racket (philosophy rule 7).
Face I attacks with heat; Face II controls with cold metal. The diagonal runs the opposite way (upper right to lower left),
so when the racket is flipped the line continues across the edge like one ribbon wrapping the frame.

| Zone | Effect | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet spot | Sand Grit (fine) over 3D Hexagon at low height | Control needs dwell and touch, so finer grit than Face I |
| Upper field | Shiny + Carbon Texture Visible, 16K aluminised in cobalt | The metallic weave is the selling point; no relief to hide it |
| Lower field | Matte navy lacquer, flat | Quiet zone that makes the aluminised field glow |
| 13 mm border | Shiny | Same bezel as Face I |
| Cut line | Laser Silver Hot Stamping stripe | Cold counterpart to Face I's gold |
| NORA wordmark | Laser (holographic), cool shift (ice blue to violet to silver) | Same hero, same position |
| "II" and "CONTROL" | Gold hot stamping, small | The one warm accent, links back to Face I |
| Throat | 3D + Shiny + Carbon Visible | Texture carried down from Face I |
| Frame side B | Matte black with a gloss cobalt pinstripe | Mirror of Face I's crimson pinstripe |

Palette 60/30/10: navy + aluminised silver / cobalt #1B6FB0 / foil (silver + cool holo, one gold touch).
