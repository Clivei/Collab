# Brand research for NORA Gemini v2

`brand_collage.jpg` puts NORA Gemini v2 next to ten 2026 rackets from Bullpadel, NOX, StarVie, Siux,
Oxdog, Babolat, Adidas and Head. `brand_specs.json` holds the data and `brand_collage.py` redraws the sheet.

**Limits.** Four research agents ran in parallel, but the sandbox only allows web search, so no product
photos or retailer pages could be opened. Every racket in the collage is an illustration drawn on the OXDOG mould
from search-result summaries (finish, colour, graphic idea), not the real artwork. Colourways differ by retailer,
and several graphics are marked unconfirmed. Quad has one retailer listing (White Tiger, "3K Double Carbon") and no design info.
Adidas and Head entries come from my own earlier web searches (the Adidas/Head agent had not reported when this was built).

## Adidas / Head agent report (received late, still search snippets only)
- Metalbone Carbon 2026 is listed as 6K carbon, rough texture, diamond, 38 mm (Padelful, retailer sheets). The plain Metalbone 2026 (Ale Galan) is listed as aluminised carbon. The earlier "16K" figure for Adidas could not be confirmed, so the sheet now says "aluminised carbon / rough texture".
- Coello Pro 2026: Extreme Spin texture, carbon + fibreglass hybrid, red Power Foam core. Colourway sources conflict.
- Sources: [Padelful Metalbone Carbon](https://www.padelful.com/en/rackets/adidas-metalbone-carbon-2026), [Padel.fyi Metalbone 2026](https://www.padel.fyi/rackets/metalbone-2026/), [Padelful Coello Pro](https://www.padelful.com/en/rackets/head-coello-pro-2026)
